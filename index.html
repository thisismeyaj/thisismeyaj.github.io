Questions

How fast will I get my staged photos?

Same day for most orders. Previews usually arrive within hours, and the full photos arrive automatically right after you pay.

When do I pay?

After you see your watermarked previews. You pay securely by card through Stripe, and the full-resolution photos are emailed to you automatically.

What photos work best?

Well-lit photos of empty interior rooms, as JPEG, PNG or WebP. On iPhone, set Settings > Camera > Formats to "Most Compatible".

Can you remove damage or change the room?

No. We only add furniture and decor, so buyers see the property as it really is.

Do I need to disclose virtual staging?

Yes. Most MLSs and many states require it. Every photo is labeled, and we include your originals.

How does the first-photo-free offer work?

It's a new-customer promotion for virtual staging: on your first order, one photo is free. It doesn't apply to design services.

Terms & privacy

Your files

By sending photos or files, you confirm you own them or have permission to use them. We use them only to complete your order. We never sell or share them for any other purpose, and we delete them on request.

How we make staged images

We use AI image tools, including Google's, to add furniture, followed by an automated quality check. Staged images include an invisible digital watermark from the AI provider and a visible "Virtually Staged" label.

Your listing

You're responsible for disclosing virtual staging in your listings as your MLS and local rules require, and for keeping the label on the photos.

Payment

Prices are in US dollars. Virtual staging is priced per photo by order size, as shown above. Design services are quoted before any work begins. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We don't see or store your card details.
